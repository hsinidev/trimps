### Game Details & Guide
Trimps: A Unique Browser Game



Trips is a browser game that combines elements of farming simulation, strategy, and exploration. Players take on the role of a Trimp, a small creature with unique abilities, as they navigate through a procedurally generated world.



Game Overview


In Trimps, you must gather resources, build structures, and defend against hostile creatures to progress through levels. The game features a variety of environments, each with its own challenges and rewards.



How to Play



To play Trims, simply open the game in your preferred browser. The controls are intuitive and easy to use, with a focus on keyboard navigation.
